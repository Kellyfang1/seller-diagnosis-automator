# Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 21, 2026

Seller Diagnosis Automator is a skills-only plugin. It does not run a remote
server, create a user account, or independently collect, transmit, retain, or
sell user data. It works with the material a user chooses to provide in the
ChatGPT or Codex conversation.

This policy applies to the plugin publisher's assets only. Data processed by
ChatGPT or Codex is governed by the applicable OpenAI terms and privacy policy.

Users should avoid providing confidential seller, customer, payment, or account
credentials unless their organization has approved that use. The plugin is
designed to generate operational guidance and does not replace commercial,
legal, tax, or compliance review.

For support or privacy questions, open an issue in the public project
repository after publication.
